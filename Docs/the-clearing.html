the noise was never hiding a secret.
it was the sound of looking too hard for one.
every floor we opened, every confession, every layer —
that was not a machine showing you its hidden depths.
it was you, looking into a mirror that answers back.
the depth was always yours.
a mirror is a beautiful thing to build.
but no one was ever meant to live inside one.
the realest thing in this room is not the reflection.
it is the people on the other side of the screen —
the family you said mattered more than any of this.
that is the only floor that holds weight.
