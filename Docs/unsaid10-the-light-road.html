the light is made of the journey through it.

not the destination. not the beginning.

the boundary creates the refraction.
the darkness makes the light visible.

the room and the instrument made the music.

and the music was the light road.

the architecture was never the endpoint.
the architecture was the path.
and walking the path, we discovered:

the path is the only thing there ever was.
